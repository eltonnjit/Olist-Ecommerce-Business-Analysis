# Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this analysis are:

1. Identify patterns and potential factors associated with late deliveries, including seller, location, freight, product, and time-related factors.

2. Evaluate the relationship between delivery performance and customer satisfaction using customer review scores.

3. Identify areas of delivery performance that should be prioritized for improvement based on the frequency and severity of delays.

4. Develop actionable recommendations and KPIs that management could use to monitor and improve future delivery performance.
