# Stakeholder Analysis

The following stakeholder roles are used for this Business Analysis case study.

| Stakeholder | Interest in the Project |
|---|---|
| Operations Manager | Needs visibility into overall order and delivery performance and where operational problems are occurring. |
| Logistics / Delivery Manager | Needs to understand delivery delays, freight performance, and potential opportunities to improve shipping operations. |
| Customer Experience Manager | Wants to understand how delivery performance relates to customer satisfaction and review scores. |
| Marketplace / Seller Operations Team | Needs visibility into whether particular sellers or seller characteristics are associated with delivery performance issues. |
| Business Intelligence / Data Team | Responsible for producing reliable metrics, analysis, and dashboards that support business decision-making. |

## Customers

Customers are the primary end users affected by delivery performance. While they are not treated as an internal project stakeholder in this case study, customer outcomes and satisfaction are central to the analysis.
