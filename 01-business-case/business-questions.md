# Business Questions

The analysis will focus on answering the following questions:

1. What percentage of completed orders were delivered later than their estimated delivery date?

2. Which sellers, geographic areas, or other order characteristics are associated with higher late-delivery rates?

3. How does delivery performance vary over time, and are there periods where delays become more common?

4. What relationship exists between late deliveries and customer review scores?

5. Which factors appear most strongly associated with poor delivery performance and should be prioritized for further investigation?

## Analytical Principle

The analysis will distinguish between correlation and causation. Patterns identified in the dataset will be described as associations unless sufficient evidence exists to establish a causal relationship.
