# Stakeholder Requirements

## Overview

Because this is an independent portfolio case study, the following stakeholder requirements are simulated based on the project's business problem and the available Olist dataset. They do not represent requirements provided by Olist.

The purpose of this requirements-gathering exercise is to identify what information hypothetical business stakeholders would need to evaluate delivery performance and customer satisfaction.

## Operations Manager

### Business Need
The Operations Manager needs an overall view of order fulfillment and delivery performance to identify areas where operational issues may exist.

### Requirements
- Monitor total completed orders and late deliveries.
- Measure the overall on-time delivery rate.
- Compare delivery performance across different time periods.
- Identify areas experiencing unusually high rates of late deliveries.

## Logistics / Delivery Manager

### Business Need
The Logistics / Delivery Manager needs to understand where delivery delays are occurring and what characteristics are associated with poor delivery performance.

### Requirements
- Compare estimated delivery dates with actual delivery dates.
- Measure the frequency and severity of delivery delays.
- Analyze delivery performance across geographic areas.
- Evaluate freight and shipping-related characteristics associated with delays.

## Customer Experience Manager

### Business Need
The Customer Experience Manager needs to understand whether delivery performance is associated with customer satisfaction.

### Requirements
- Compare customer review scores between on-time and late orders.
- Measure average review scores by delivery performance.
- Identify whether severe delays are associated with lower customer ratings.

## Marketplace / Seller Operations Team

### Business Need
The Marketplace / Seller Operations Team needs visibility into seller-level delivery performance.

### Requirements
- Compare late-delivery rates across sellers.
- Identify sellers associated with unusually high levels of delayed orders.
- Evaluate whether seller location or other seller characteristics are associated with delivery performance.

## Business Intelligence / Data Team

### Business Need
The BI / Data Team needs a reliable reporting solution that allows business stakeholders to monitor delivery performance.

### Requirements
- Establish consistent definitions for key delivery KPIs.
- Provide filters for relevant dimensions such as time period and geography.
- Ensure metrics are calculated consistently from validated source data.
- Present delivery and customer satisfaction metrics through an interactive dashboard.
