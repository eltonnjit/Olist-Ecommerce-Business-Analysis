# User Stories

## Overview

The following user stories translate the project's business and functional requirements into stakeholder-focused needs.

These user stories were created for this independent portfolio case study and do not represent official requirements provided by Olist.

## User Stories

### US-001 — Monitor Overall Delivery Performance

**As an Operations Manager,**  
I want to view overall delivery performance metrics  
**so that** I can monitor the health of the fulfillment process and identify performance issues.

**Related Requirements:** BR-001, FR-002

---

### US-002 — Identify Late Deliveries

**As a Logistics / Delivery Manager,**  
I want orders to be classified as on-time or late  
**so that** I can measure delivery performance consistently.

**Related Requirements:** BR-001, BR-002, FR-001

---

### US-003 — Measure Delay Severity

**As a Logistics / Delivery Manager,**  
I want to see how many days late delayed orders were delivered  
**so that** I can distinguish minor delays from more severe delivery issues.

**Related Requirements:** BR-002, FR-003

---

### US-004 — Analyze Delivery Trends Over Time

**As an Operations Manager,**  
I want to analyze delivery performance across different time periods  
**so that** I can identify trends or periods with unusually high levels of delayed orders.

**Related Requirements:** BR-003, FR-004

---

### US-005 — Compare Geographic Performance

**As a Logistics / Delivery Manager,**  
I want to compare delivery performance across geographic areas  
**so that** I can identify locations associated with higher late-delivery rates.

**Related Requirements:** BR-004, FR-005

---

### US-006 — Evaluate Seller Performance

**As a Marketplace / Seller Operations stakeholder,**  
I want to compare delivery performance across sellers  
**so that** I can identify sellers associated with unusually high late-delivery rates.

**Related Requirements:** BR-005, FR-006

---

### US-007 — Understand Customer Satisfaction

**As a Customer Experience Manager,**  
I want to compare review scores between on-time and late deliveries  
**so that** I can understand how delivery performance is associated with customer satisfaction.

**Related Requirements:** BR-006, FR-007

---

### US-008 — Analyze Freight and Order Characteristics

**As a Logistics / Delivery Manager,**  
I want to analyze freight and order characteristics alongside delivery outcomes  
**so that** I can identify patterns associated with poor delivery performance.

**Related Requirements:** BR-007, FR-008

---

### US-009 — Filter Performance Data

**As a Business Stakeholder,**  
I want to filter delivery metrics by relevant dimensions such as time period, geography, and seller  
**so that** I can investigate specific areas of performance.

**Related Requirements:** BR-003, BR-004, BR-005, BR-009, FR-009

---

### US-010 — View an Interactive Dashboard

**As a Business Stakeholder,**  
I want an interactive Power BI dashboard containing delivery and customer satisfaction KPIs  
**so that** I can quickly understand performance and support business decisions.

**Related Requirements:** BR-009, FR-010

---

### US-011 — Ensure Reliable Metrics

**As a BI / Data Team stakeholder,**  
I want source data to be validated before KPI calculations are performed  
**so that** dashboard metrics are based on reliable data.

**Related Requirements:** BR-009, FR-011

---

### US-012 — Review Business Findings

**As a Business Stakeholder,**  
I want documented findings and recommendations based on the analysis  
**so that** I can prioritize areas for further investigation and process improvement.

**Related Requirements:** BR-008, BR-010, FR-012
