# Business Requirements

## Overview

The following business requirements translate the simulated stakeholder needs into measurable objectives for the Olist E-Commerce Delivery Performance & Process Improvement project.

These requirements were developed for this independent portfolio case study and do not represent official requirements provided by Olist.

## Business Requirements

### BR-001 — Monitor Overall Delivery Performance
The business must be able to monitor overall delivery performance, including total completed orders, on-time deliveries, late deliveries, and on-time delivery rate.

### BR-002 — Measure Delivery Delays
The business must be able to compare estimated and actual delivery dates to determine the frequency and severity of delivery delays.

### BR-003 — Analyze Delivery Performance Over Time
The business must be able to evaluate delivery performance across different time periods to identify trends or periods with unusually high levels of delayed orders.

### BR-004 — Evaluate Geographic Delivery Performance
The business must be able to compare delivery performance across available geographic areas to identify locations associated with higher late-delivery rates.

### BR-005 — Evaluate Seller Delivery Performance
The business must be able to compare delivery performance across sellers and identify sellers associated with unusually high rates of delayed orders.

### BR-006 — Evaluate Customer Satisfaction
The business must be able to analyze the relationship between delivery performance and customer review scores.

### BR-007 — Analyze Freight and Order Characteristics
The business must be able to evaluate available freight and order characteristics to identify patterns associated with poor delivery performance.

### BR-008 — Identify Improvement Opportunities
The business must be able to identify and prioritize areas of delivery performance that may benefit from further investigation or process improvement.

### BR-009 — Provide Performance Reporting
Business stakeholders must have access to a reporting solution that communicates key delivery and customer satisfaction metrics in a clear and consistent manner.

### BR-010 — Support Data-Driven Decision Making
The analysis must provide findings and recommendations that stakeholders can use to support decisions related to delivery performance and customer experience.
